# Portfolio presentation

[View the slides (PDF)](TikTok%20-%20Portfolio%20Overview.pdf) · [Download the editable PowerPoint](TikTok%20-%20Portfolio%20Overview.pptx)

An overview of TikTok’s reported decision automation in the Protection of minors category, comparing January–May in 2024, 2025 and 2026.

The nine slides cover the research question, methods, findings, dashboard, institutional context and limitations. Company statements and employees’ accounts are attributed; the data does not establish effects of layoffs or child-safety outcomes.

This learning project used AI assistance. Python, MySQL and Tableau were combined deliberately for practice. A fresh end-to-end rerun remains unverified.
